# ⬟ Blueprint: The "Pro" Requirement Template

A "Proper" requirement is more than just a list of features. To get the absolute best out of Blueprint's 8 specialized agents, your input should follow the **Context-Constraint-Outcome (CCO)** framework.

---

## 🏗️ The CCO Framework

1.  **Context**: What is the business domain? Why are we building this now? Who are the users?
2.  **Constraints**: What technologies ARE mandatory? What should we specifically AVOID? (e.g., "No external auth providers", "Must work offline").
3.  **Outcomes**: What does success look like? (e.g., "Must handle 10k concurrent orders").

---

## 🌟 The "Golden" Project Input (Copy-Paste)

Use this professionally crafted requirement for your demonstrations. It is designed to trigger all 8 phases of Blueprint with maximum depth.

### Project: **"Lumina: The Intelligent Energy Grid"**

**The Brief**:
> "Lumina is a next-generation Energy Management System for metropolitan smart grids. Our current problem is 'Grid Instability' caused by the unpredictable surge of EV charging and residential solar feed-in. We need an intelligent orchestrator to balance the load.
>
> **Core Requirements**:
> 1. **Live Grid Map**: A real-time visualization of energy consumption across 5 city sectors using a geo-spatial dashboard.
> 2. **Surge Prediction (WOW FACTOR)**: Analyze historical consumption patterns + local weather data to predict 'Grid Stress Events' 6 hours in advance with 90% accuracy.
> 3. **Dynamic Pricing Engine**: A backend logic that auto-adjusts kW/h prices based on live demand to incentivize off-peak charging.
> 4. **User Portal**: Residential users must see their 'Green Score' and receive 'Nudge Notifications' when clean energy is abundant.
> 5. **Admin Contract**: A high-performance API for utility providers to override surge controls.
>
> **Technical Preferences**:
> - We prioritize **FastAPI** for its high-async throughput and **React (Framer Motion)** for the fluid dashboard experience.
> - Security: Strict OAuth2 with RBAC (Admin, Provider, Resident)."

---

## 🔬 Why This Works (The Blueprint Alchemy)

When you pass the above requirement to Blueprint, here is what happens behind the scenes:

-   **Phase 1 (Identity)**: Sees "Smart Grids" and "Sustainable Energy." It will likely suggest a "Digital Neon" or "High-Tech Nature" brand with vibrant greens and sleek dark modes.
-   **Phase 2 (Scope)**: Sees "Surge Prediction" and "Nudge Notifications." It will identify these as **Intelligent Capabilities**, creating a full spec for the prediction logic and the nudging trigger.
-   **Phase 3 (Skeleton)**: Sees "Geo-spatial dashboard." It will structure the project to include libraries like `Leaflet` or `Mapbox` early in the tree.
-   **Phase 6 (Data)**: Sees "Live demand" and "kW/h prices." It knows this is time-series data and will architect the database models to handle high-frequency updates.
-   **Phase 8 (Handoff)**: Synthesizes all the above into a 4,000-word master brief that allows an AI coder to build the *entire* dashboard and pricing engine in one go.
